The five palettes

Shared navy ramp, five different accents. Ratios are measured on the pairs that actually carry meaning: body text, secondary text, links, and button fills. AA needs 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text and UI borders; AAA needs 7:1.

The six type pairings

Each set with identical content so you're comparing the typefaces, not the words. Ordered from most expressive to most minimal — the lower ones are the safer answer to "clean, not over the top".
