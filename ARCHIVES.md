# Recent Years (event journals)

## RI Year 2024/25 — "Magic of Rotary"
DIR: Damian De Cruz. Header image: `images/archive-logos/archive-2024-25-header.jpg`

**Growth:** Started with 131 Interact clubs; grew to **142 clubs (9 new charters)** by May 2025. Notable: chartering of the **Interact Club of the School for the Blind** (support from PP Menuli Perera). 30+ club orientations; 11 District Meetings; largest-ever Interflash and Race4Change.

**District Meetings (11):**
1. Jul 28 — St. Joseph's College
2. Aug 25 — Zahira College
3. Sep 15 — Lyceum International School, Nugegoda
4. Oct 12 — incl. Pinktober Initiative segment
5. Nov 2 — D.S. Senanayake College (Diwali)
6. Dec 14 — Nawaloka Auditorium (Christmas)
7. Jan 5 — St. Peter's College (Interaction Opening Ceremony)
8. Feb 16 — Hillwood College, Kandy
9. Mar 9 — Rosewood Ceylon, Dehiwala (Iftar)
10. Apr — National Arts Academy, Pallekele (Avurudu)
11. May — St. Joseph's College

**Major events & initiatives:**
- **Interact Board Training Seminar (IBTS)** — Aug 4, Mahanama College; 800+ Interactors from all zones.
- **60 Years of Interact Commemoration** — visit to St. John's College, Jaffna (first Interact Club in the country).
- **Project Nuware** — renovation of Weerakatiya Madya Maha Vidyalaya, Matale.
- **Clean Up Sri Lanka** — 28 Sep 2024, organised with Shraddha TV.
- **Interact Week Celebrations (IWC)** — 7 initiatives across 5 zones (Colombo, Kandy, Kurunegala, Galle, Ratnapura).
- **GreenCon '24** — Nov 9; planted 500 trees at Halwathura Estate, Horana; convention at St. Joseph's College, 100+ participants.
- **Everyone Matters** — Phase 1 (Dec 4, Child Action Lanka, Colombo); Phase 2 (eight clusters across regions).
- **LiveLight** — Jan 4, Trace Expert City; talent/creativity showcase.
- **Interaction Colombo** — Jan 12–13, Ralla Beach & St. Joseph's College; 24 clubs. Winners: D.S. Senanayake College & Visakha Vidyalaya.
- **Interaction Hill Country** — Jan 18, 25 & Feb 4; three winning clubs.
- **ILT Awards Night** — Jan 31, Royal Institute Campus.
- **34th Interact District Conference (IDC)** — Mar 22, Eagles' Lakeside, Mount Lavinia; Jezon Fernando elected DIRE.
- **Interflash** — Apr 5, Lotus Tower; 12,000+ attendees; major income for Rotary Little Hearts Foundation; chaired by Int. Yumeth Ratnayake.
- **Race4Change (R4C)** — concluded May 18; 1,000+ runners; for Little Hearts Foundation.
- **Intercede** — flagship community service; raised LKR 1.7M for Hethersett Tamil Vidyalayam, Nuwara Eliya (roof reconstruction + stationery).
- **Interlude '25** — Rotary–Interact collaboration; seven clusters; chaired by Int. Rovin Mohotti.
- **Project RISE UP** — mentorship pairing new/developing clubs with established ones.
- **District RYLA 2025** — May 24, Carey College Colombo; 200+ Interactors; emotional intelligence, goal-setting, financial literacy, AI ethics. Best RYLARIAN 2025: Int. Husni Habeeb (Zahira College).
